### 🛡️ EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
**Trustia AI** develops the core autonomy operating software for Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs) and passenger electric vehicles operating in **GPS-denied, electronic-warfare-heavy, and dense urban environments**. 

While vehicle manufacturers build robust physical hardware, they lack the proprietary software stack required for true commercial autonomy. Trustia bridges this gap by providing a **hardware-agnostic, zero-external-dependency software brain** that integrates centimeter-level 3D SLAM, kinematic trajectory planning, multi-modal IED/mine AI detection, and drive-by-wire actuation into any vehicle via standard CAN-FD and ROS 2 protocols.
