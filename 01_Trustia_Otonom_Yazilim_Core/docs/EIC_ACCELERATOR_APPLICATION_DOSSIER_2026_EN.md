## 📝 2. EIC STEP 1 SHORT APPLICATION — OFFICIAL PROPOSAL TEXTS

### 🔬 Q1: Breakthrough Innovation & Problem Statement (Why EIC?)
> **Executive Narrative:**  
> Autonomous unmanned systems operating in contested electronic warfare (EW) environments, underground tunnels, disaster zones, and dense urban canyons face a fatal vulnerability: **complete reliance on GNSS/GPS infrastructure and proprietary, black-box AI algorithms**. When satellite signals are jammed or spoofed, existing commercial autonomous platforms experience immediate sensor disorientation and catastrophic mission failure.
>
> **The TRUSTIA Breakthrough:**  
> TRUSTIA introduces a paradigm shift in autonomous vehicle intelligence: a **100% zero-external-dependency, certifiable, deterministic software brain** engineered specifically for GPS-denied tactical and dual-use operations. Built with mathematical rigour, TRUSTIA unifies:
> 1. **Continuous 3D Pose Graph SLAM & Visual Odometry:** Maintaining sub-10cm drift without satellite connectivity through LiDAR scan-matching and optical feature tracking.
> 2. **Multi-Modal AI Threat Detection & 30m Auto-Quarantine:** Real-time sensor fusion (thermal anomaly + metal induction + Ground Penetrating Radar - GPR) detecting improvised explosive devices (IEDs), landmines, and chemical/radiological (CBRN) hazards before physical contact.
> 3. **Hybrid Air-Ground Swarm Intelligence:** Autonomous distributed tactical formation control (Wedge, Column, Diamond) between UGVs and UAVs.
> 4. **Military-Grade Determinism & Standards:** Out-of-the-box compliance with SAE AS6009/AS6091 JAUS, NATO STANAG 4586, ROS 2, and CAN FD.
